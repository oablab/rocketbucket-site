What we learned building RocketBucket, written down while it was still fresh.

September 8, 2026

500 GB went up. The app said it wasn't sure.

Resumable multipart uploads to S3 and R2 at 500 GB: a 14.5 GB memory blow-up, an O(n²) journal, and a server that needed thirty seconds to glue 9,847 pieces together.
