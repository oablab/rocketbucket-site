What we learned building RocketBucket, written down while it was still fresh.

November 3, 2026

One byte at a time: why downloads were 28× slower than uploads, and why a thousand small files waited in line

Many files at once took uploading 1,000 files of 100 KB from 10.05 s to 0.72 s; handling each chunk as a whole took a 1 GiB download from 31 s to 0.48 s. These improvements ship in the next release, 0.3.0.
September 8, 2026

500 GB to R2: what happens when a huge upload stops, and when it starts again

Multipart upload and resume in RocketBucket: 9,847 parts, a journal that outlives the app, zero re-reads on resume, and a completion proof that waits for R2 to assemble. Peak memory about 900 MB to 1 GB.
